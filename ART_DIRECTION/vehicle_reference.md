# Vehicle visual reference

Primary visual reference supplied by the project owner:
- 2026 Ferrari SF-26 on Sketchfab: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2026-ferrari-sf-26-e5ca6cecdc42449283f4bed27360f2a7

## ELDEN 1 modelling target
Use this reference for modern 2026-era Formula car proportions and overall design language. The in-game vehicle must be an original, unbranded design unless explicit redistribution and derivative-work permissions are confirmed for the source model.

### Required visible details
- Proper continuous halo structure with front pillar and side supports.
- Open cockpit and correctly proportioned driver seating area.
- Sculpted monocoque and nose transition.
- Front and rear wings with layered elements and endplates.
- Exposed front and rear suspension wishbones.
- Realistic wheel, tyre, brake and hub proportions.
- Floor edge, sidepod undercut and rear diffuser geometry.
- High-quality materials, decals, panel seams and realistic lighting.

## Asset approval checklist
Do not import a downloaded model until its licence and redistribution rights are checked. Inspect the actual downloadable mesh in a 3D viewer/editor; a Sketchfab thumbnail alone does not verify topology, halo geometry, materials, or rigging. Do not claim the asset is integrated until it has been added to the project and tested in-engine.
